Serving Osceola County

Two markets.
One standard.

Kissimmee runs two separate housing markets side by side. On the residential side — neighborhoods like Celebration, Kindred, and the older blocks around downtown — you've got full-time homeowners dealing with the same plumbing leaks, fence repairs, and electrical quirks as anywhere else in Central Florida. On the other side, the US-192 corridor and the neighborhoods off Osceola Parkway hold tens of thousands of short-term rentals that need consistent upkeep between guest stays.

We work both. For homeowners, that means straight estimates and same-week scheduling. For property managers and STR owners, it means we can coordinate directly with your cleaning crew, work within checkout-to-checkin windows, and turn around small repairs fast so you don't miss a booking. We keep notes on each property and can handle recurring maintenance across multiple units if that's what you need.

We serve Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Celebration, and Poinciana — the full Osceola County footprint. Call for an estimate or fill out the form below. No service fees, no travel charges within our area.
