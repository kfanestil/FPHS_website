01

Who we are and what these terms cover

Fanny Pack Home Services LLC ("Fanny Pack," "we," "us") is a Florida limited liability company based in Altamonte Springs. These terms apply when you use fanny-pack.net and when we do work for you.

When you approve an estimate or book a job with us, you agree to these terms. If your written estimate, quote, invoice or a signed agreement for your job says something different, that document controls for that job.

02

Estimates and quotes

  • Phone, text-a-photo and video estimates are free.
  • If we need to see the job in person before we can price it, the on-site estimate is $85. If you book the job we quoted, the $85 is credited toward your invoice.
  • Quotes are good for 30 days unless the quote says otherwise. Material prices move, so after 30 days we may need to re-price.
  • If a job is billed by the hour, the rate and any minimum are on your estimate.

A quote covers the work it describes, based on what we could see. If we open something up and find a problem nobody could see before, like rot, water damage, a bad earlier repair, or damaged wiring or pipes, we stop, show you, and give you a new price. We do not do or bill extra work until you approve it by text, email or in person.

03

Booking, access and cancellations

  • Nothing is booked until we confirm the day and time with you by text, call or email.
  • Please give us safe access to the work area, working power and water where the job needs them, and keep pets away from where we are working. An adult (18 or older) needs to be home, or you can arrange access with us ahead of time.
  • Tell us before we start about anything we should know: known leaks, mold, damaged wiring, past repairs, or anything else that could be unsafe.
  • You are responsible for any HOA, condo board or landlord approval the job needs.
  • To cancel or reschedule, let us know at least 24 hours ahead. If we already bought special-order or custom materials for your job, you pay for those materials and we hand them over to you.
  • We may stop or decline work if the site is unsafe, if we are asked to do work outside the quote, or if we are treated abusively. You pay only for the work completed up to that point.

If a Florida or federal home-sale rule gives you the right to cancel a purchase within a set number of days, nothing in these terms takes that right away, and we will give you any notice those rules require.

04

What we do, and what we hand off

We do repair, installation and maintenance work that Florida allows without a state contractor license.

Work that legally needs a licensed electrician, plumber, HVAC contractor, roofer or general contractor, or that needs a building permit, is outside what we do. If part of your list falls into that category, we will tell you before we start and point you to a licensed pro. We do not pull permits.

Our own team does the work. On some jobs we bring a helper. If we ever want another tradesperson to handle part of your job, we will tell you who before they come out.

05

Materials

  • We can buy the materials, or you can supply them. Materials we buy are billed as shown on your estimate or invoice.
  • If you supply materials, fixtures or products, we install them as provided. We are not responsible for defects, wrong sizes, missing parts or failures in materials we did not supply. Our guarantee still covers our installation work.
  • Products and materials carry their maker's warranty. We pass along any warranty paperwork we receive. We do not add a separate warranty on products.
  • We haul off old parts, packaging and debris from our work unless the quote says otherwise. We do not haul off hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, batteries, or anything that may contain asbestos.
06

Payment

  • Payment is due when the job is done, unless your estimate or invoice gives a different due date.
  • For larger jobs, multi-day jobs or special-order materials, we may ask for a deposit of up to 50% before we schedule the work or buy materials. If so, your estimate will show it.
  • We accept the payment methods shown on your invoice. Card payments go through our payment provider, and we do not store full card numbers.
  • If an invoice goes unpaid, we may pause further work and pursue the balance through collection or small claims court.
07

Our 30-day workmanship guarantee

If something we did fails because of our workmanship within 30 days after we finish the job, call, text or email us. We will come back and fix it at no charge.

For mowing, pool and cleaning visits, tell us within 2 days of the visit and we will come back and take care of anything we missed.

What the guarantee does not cover

  • Products and materials. The maker's warranty applies to those.
  • Materials or products you supplied.
  • Damage from misuse, accidents, storms, flooding, pests or normal wear.
  • Work that someone else changes or repairs after us.
  • Conditions that existed before we started, including ones we pointed out to you.

Our fix is to repair or redo the work. If we cannot fix it, we refund what you paid for the part of the job that failed.

Please give us the chance to fix it first. We cannot reimburse another company's bill for a repair we were never asked to make.

08

Insurance, damage and limits

We carry general liability insurance. Ask and we will send you a certificate.

If you think we damaged something, tell us as soon as you notice, and give us a chance to look before it is repaired, unless it is an emergency such as an active leak.

Older homes hide things. We use a stud finder and take care when we drill, cut and fasten. If we hit something inside a wall, ceiling or floor that we could not reasonably have known was there, we will stop and tell you right away. Repairing that hidden item is your cost unless we were careless.

We are not responsible for problems caused by conditions that existed before we started, such as rot, old wiring, corroded pipes, weak framing or earlier bad repairs, unless our own carelessness made them worse.

To the extent Florida law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, such as lost rent, lost income, hotel stays or time off work. Apart from damage to property that we caused and personal injury, our total liability for any job is limited to the amount you paid us for that job.

Nothing in these terms limits our liability where Florida law does not allow it to be limited, including for gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

09

Photos of our work

We take photos before, during and after a job for our records. We may use photos of our work on this website and in our marketing. We leave out or crop out house numbers, faces, family photos and anything else that would identify you or your address. If you do not want photos of your job used, tell us and we will not use them.

If something goes wrong

Call or email us first. Most problems get fixed with one conversation, and we would rather hear about it than have you stay unhappy.

If we cannot resolve it together, these terms and any work we do for you are governed by Florida law. Any lawsuit will be filed in the state courts for the Florida county where the work was done, or in Seminole County, Florida. Either of us may use small claims court for claims that qualify.
