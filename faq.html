Licensing & insurance

Is Fanny Pack Home Services licensed and insured?

Yes. Every professional in our network is required to carry general liability insurance and workers compensation coverage before they can work with us. It is a non-negotiable requirement, not an option. You are protected if anything goes wrong on the job.

Do handymen need a license in Florida?

Florida law requires a contractor license for jobs above certain dollar thresholds and for specific trade work (electrical, plumbing, structural). Permit-free handyman work — the category most home repairs fall into — does not require a state contractor license, but professionals still need to carry insurance. Our network professionals are licensed where licensing is required and insured on every job. See our full breakdown: Do Handymen Need a License in Florida?

What happens if something gets damaged on the job?

Our professionals carry general liability insurance specifically to cover property damage that occurs during a job. If something is damaged, the insurance is there. You are not left holding the bill.

Pricing & estimates

How much does Fanny Pack Home Services charge?

Pricing depends on the job. We do not quote a flat rate without knowing what the work involves — that would not be honest. What we can tell you is that you will get a clear quote before any work starts, with no surprises on the invoice. Call us or submit a request and we will tell you exactly what it costs.

Are estimates free?

Phone and virtual estimates are free. If you need a technician to come on-site to assess the job before quoting, there is an $85 on-site estimate fee. That fee is applied toward the total cost of the job if you decide to proceed. You are not paying $85 for nothing — it goes toward the work.

Is there a minimum job size or minimum charge?

No. There is no minimum job size. A single outlet swap gets the same honest quote as a full punch list. We do not turn away small jobs.

Will the price change after the work starts?

The quoted price is the price you pay — with one exception: if we open a wall and find a problem that was not visible during the estimate (structural damage, previous bad repair work, etc.), we will show you and re-quote before touching anything new. Nothing proceeds without your approval.
