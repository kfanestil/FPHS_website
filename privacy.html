01

Who we are

This policy explains what Fanny Pack Home Services LLC ("we," "us") collects when you visit fanny-pack.net, call or text us, or hire us, and what we do with it. We are a small local business. We use your information to answer your request and do your job.

02

What we collect

What you give us

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and the address of the job.
  • Job details: what you need done, the day and time window you pick, and any photos or videos you text or email us.
  • Job records: our notes, before and after photos, estimates, invoices and payment records. We do not store full card numbers; card payments go through our payment provider.

What services that send us work give us

If you find us through a home-services marketplace or referral service, that service passes us your name, contact details and job request. Its own privacy policy covers what it does with your information.

What is collected when you visit this site

  • Google Analytics records the pages you view, how long you stay, how you found the site, your device and browser type, and your approximate location based on your IP address. We look at this as totals and trends, and we do not use it to identify you.
  • Our web host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the site and block attacks.
  • Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, icons and map code from unpkg.com, and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Your browser sends those services your IP address when it loads them.

Calls and texts

Our phone service keeps a log of calls and texts with us, including your number and the messages.

03

How we use it

  • Answer your request and give you a price.
  • Schedule, do and bill the job, and keep a record of it.
  • Check in after a job to make sure it is right, and ask once whether you would leave a review.
  • Honor our guarantee and answer warranty or insurance questions.
  • Keep business, tax and accounting records.
  • See which pages people use so we can improve the site.
  • Block spam and fraud, and follow the law.

If you have hired us before, we may now and then send a seasonal reminder, such as Christmas light hanging or a pre-storm check. Tell us to stop, or reply STOP, and we will. We do not send marketing messages to people who only asked for an estimate.

We never sell your personal information.

04

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to run the business:

  • Companies that provide our business tools and handle information for us: website hosting (Cloudflare), web forms (Formspree), email (Google Workspace), phone and text (Google Voice), scheduling and job management (ServiceM8), invoicing and bookkeeping (Zoho Books), payment processing, website analytics (Google Analytics), and AI assistant software we use for office work such as organizing job notes and drafting messages.
  • People working on your job, such as a helper, get what they need to do it: your name, the job address and the job details. If we ever bring in another tradesperson, we will tell you first.
  • Our insurance company, accountant or attorney, when needed for a claim, our books or legal advice.
  • Government agencies or others when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.
  • A buyer, if we ever sell or reorganize the business. They would have to follow this policy for information we collected under it.

We do not share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

05

Calls and text messages

When you give us your phone number on a form, by text or on a call, you are asking us to contact you. We will call or text you about your request and your job: questions, quotes, scheduling, arrival times, invoices and follow-up.

Message frequency depends on your job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text and we will stop texting you. For help, reply HELP or call (904) 468-7667.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

06

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies in your browser to count visits and see how people use the site. Google receives your IP address and the pages you visit. Google explains what it does with that data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

To opt out, install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker. The site works without analytics cookies.

We do not place advertising or remarketing tags on this site, and we do not use cookies to show you ads.

Browsers send Do Not Track signals inconsistently, and this site does not respond to them. The opt-out options above work regardless.

07

How long we keep it

  • Estimate requests that never turn into a job: we delete them within about two years.
  • Job records, invoices and payment records: as long as we need them for warranty questions, and as long as tax and business record rules require, usually several years.
  • Website analytics: kept in Google Analytics for the limited period set in our account.
08

How we protect it

We keep customer information in password-protected business accounts and limit access to the people who need it to do the work. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

09

Your choices

You can ask us to:

  • Send you a copy of the information we have about you.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete it. We will delete what we can; we may need to keep invoices and payment records for tax and legal reasons.
  • Stop texts (reply STOP) or emails (reply and tell us).

Email hello@fanny-pack.net or call (904) 468-7667. We will respond within 30 days, and we may ask you to confirm who you are first.

10

Children

This site and our services are for adults arranging work on their homes. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already have, we will tell past customers directly.

12

Contact

Questions about your information? Reach us here. Our terms cover estimates, payment and our guarantee.
